# WP16 Field-First Design Standard

## Context
The platform must work in bright sun, dust, mud, noise, fatigue, gloves, and unstable connectivity.

## Field-first rules
- Light mode by default.
- High contrast on every critical label and action.
- Minimum large touch targets for primary controls.
- No dependency on hover for essential actions.
- Mobile layouts must stack cleanly without horizontal scrolling.
- Tables must degrade into readable mobile cards.

## Ergonomic standards
- Primary buttons must be visually dominant and glove-friendly.
- Inputs must minimize typing and expose large labels.
- Sticky mobile affordances must not cover the content they control.
- Photos are evidence, not decoration.
- Loading states should reassure operators that the system is working.

## Visual constraints
- No muddy transparency for core shell surfaces.
- No decorative glass where readability suffers.
- No low-contrast tint-on-tint states.
- No tiny meta text for critical guidance.

## Trust and reliability
- Offline or delayed conditions must be explained in operator language.
- Success states must confirm what changed.
- Validation messages must explain what happened, why, and what to do next.
