## Meaningful gaps (candidates for future tracks)
- **Group member removal API** — every competitor with recipient groups supports member removal; ours currently requires per-recipient deactivate. Track 19.50-follow-up candidate (single small endpoint).
- **Domain sparkline in Cockpit cards** — HammerTech and Procore render a mini 8-week score curve inside their equivalent widgets. Cheap to add on top of our existing `/history` endpoint.
- **Slack / Teams webhook delivery** — competitors ship equivalent digests to chat channels. Only if MASCI adopts one of those tools; otherwise noise.

## Verdict
MASCI OI is competitive on every dimension that changes leadership decisions. The universal 0-100 score, WoW-delta Weekly Operations report, honest insufficient-data path, and no-auto-decision doctrine are **distinctive advantages** — none of the ten competitors combine all four.

Ignoring cosmetic differences, MASCI OI is **at or above the industry line for operational decision quality** and is ready for MASCI executive rollout.
