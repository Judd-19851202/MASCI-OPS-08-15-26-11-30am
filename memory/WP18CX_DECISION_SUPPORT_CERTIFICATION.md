# WP18CX Decision Support Certification

## Constitutional target
Each major operator-facing surface should quickly answer:
1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. Is it important?
4. What should I do?
5. Who owns it?
6. When must it be resolved?
7. What evidence supports the recommendation?

## Runtime-backed passes
- `Project Performance` recommendation cards expose owner, source, evidence, confidence, and drill-down path.
- PM `Project Schedule` and `Project Budget` pages now use action-language like `Items needing review`, `Proposed updates`, `Receipts needing review`, and `PO links needing review`.
- Safety / Dispatch / Shop / HR / Field Leadership hubs each orient the operator toward the next queue or action.

## Runtime-backed partials
- Daily Report AI summary sections exist in runtime, but full seeded AI-generated coaching output was not directly certified.
- Executive-family role sessions are strong on shared surfaces, but not fully isolated per role.

## Final result
`PASS` for audited decision-support web surfaces.
