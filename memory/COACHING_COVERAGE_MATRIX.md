## Coaching evaluation framework (14 questions)
1. Does coaching exist?
2. Is the coaching operational (vs software)?
3. Is the coaching calm (vs heavy/instructional)?
4. Is the coaching in the correct location?
5. Is the coaching weight appropriate (not too heavy)?
6. Is the coaching present (not missing)?
7. Is the coaching current (not outdated)?
8. Is the coaching bilingual (EN+ES)?
9. Does it explain downstream continuity?
10. Does it explain "what happens next"?
11. Does it explain operational ownership?
12. Does it reinforce doctrine?
13. Does it avoid software language?
14. Does it match current platform tone?

- Articles missing `why` block: **27 / 137 (20%)** — mostly `role-*` stubs and `task-*`/`tshoot-*` quickhelp
- Articles missing `next` block: **69 / 137 (50%)** — same families dominate
- All 7 iter414 DLS articles carry full 5-block pattern ✅
- All iter317/iter319 LifecycleGuide-pattern articles carry full pattern ✅

**Why this is non-blocking**: The shorter articles (`task-*`, `tshoot-*`) are intentionally compact — they answer one question quickly. Forcing 5-block on them would violate restraint. The legitimate gaps are the 9 `role-*` articles which arguably should carry "what happens next?" closing blocks.
