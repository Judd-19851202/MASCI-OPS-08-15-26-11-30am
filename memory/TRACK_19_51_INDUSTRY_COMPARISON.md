## MASCI-specific distinctive posture
- **11 cross-domain OI products** on one engine — none of the competitors have a Weekly Operations WoW-delta report or a Corporate weighted rollup.
- **No-auto-decision doctrine** — leadership never sees an automatic verdict on fault, discipline, or preventability. Unique.
- **Insufficient-data honesty** — never fakes a score. Unique.
- **Sandboxed HTML preview in the admin cockpit** — unique.

## What MASCI can borrow (in priority order)
1. **HCSS / Motive daily-briefing panel** — a single "here's your day" strip at the top of Field / Superintendent home.
2. **Fleetio DVIR-first hub layout** — clean model for the Fleet Visibility redesign.
3. **Fieldwire task-attention hierarchy** — informs the Attention Strip visual grammar.
4. **HammerTech safety observation flow** — the Safety Hub Attention Strip should feel this direct.

## What MASCI should NOT borrow
- Procore's notification firehose (already avoided).
- Samsara's real-time overload dashboards (already avoided).
- SafetyCulture's inspection-template gallery (out of scope).

## Verdict
MASCI OI is already ahead on decision quality. The remaining gap is **portal-home consistency** — every hub should feel like the OI Cockpit. That gap is what this track's Remediation Roadmap closes.
