# WP18 ECAP Implementation Sequence

Date: 2026-08-03

## Final sequence decision

**Decision label:** `APPROVED_CONSTITUTIONAL_DECISION`  
**Proof label:** `DOCUMENTED_ONLY`

WP-18C must execute in dependency order, not visual preference order.

Every package in the sequence below inherits the WP-17 Product Constitution, the WP-18 ECAP, and the WP-18 Operational Intelligence Constitution. Compliance is mandatory.

## Exact sequence

1. **WP-18C1 — Enterprise Hierarchy Foundation**  
   propagate accepted hierarchy and inheritance rules
2. **WP-18C2 — Authority and Source-of-Truth Enforcement**  
   resource federation, notifications, approvals, source boundaries
3. **WP-18C3 — Budget Hierarchy Foundation**  
   establish budget baseline / revision / commitment / actual-cost trust model
4. **WP-18C4 — Cost-Code and Estimate Mapping**  
   connect cost-code planning to budget and estimate mapping
5. **WP-18C5 — Schedule / Lookahead / Actuals Spine**  
   govern Daily Report → progress → schedule / quantity / constraint flow
6. **WP-18C6 — Production and Quantity Intelligence**  
   accepted quantity, productivity, cost-per-unit, production trust
7. **WP-18C7 — Forecasting and Commitments**  
   ETC / EAC / commitments / forecast rollups
8. **WP-18C8 — Earned Value Engine**  
   only after budget, schedule, quantity, and actual-cost trust lines are live
9. **WP-18C9 — Executive and Portfolio Intelligence**  
   reporting hierarchy, KPI dictionary, ODS refactor, legacy digest retirement
10. **WP-18C10 — Migration, Backfill, Reconciliation, and Certification**  
   final cutover, historical reconciliation, acceptance evidence

## Sequence law

Budget precedes EV.  
Hierarchy and authority precede financial and reporting implementation.  
Migration and certification close the sequence; they do not occur as an afterthought.

No step is constitutionally complete unless it creates downstream value, reduces duplicate entry where applicable, and makes the platform measurably smarter than before the step began.
