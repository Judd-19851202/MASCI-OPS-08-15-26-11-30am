## Where MASCI already exceeds industry

- **No-auto-decision doctrine** — every platform above will auto-classify recordability/fault/liability. MASCI explicitly does NOT.
- **Universal Operational Intelligence Score** — the 0–100 explainable score with named contributors is unique across the industry (most competitors offer arbitrary "health" chips with black-box scoring).
- **Zero-drift additive engine** — no competitor ships an engine that guarantees legacy behaviour on every release.
- **Cutover doctrine** — env-flag reversible cutover gates for retiring legacy systems.
