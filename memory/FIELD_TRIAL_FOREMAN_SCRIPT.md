# FIELD TRIAL — FOREMAN SCRIPT
## Plain-language instructions · print or read on phone

**Time required**: 5–10 minutes per excavation record · 3 days
**Devices**: Use your phone the way you normally would.
**Language**: Use English or Spanish — your choice. Pick whichever the form is on right now, or switch at the top.

---

## DAY 1 · ONE DAILY REPORT + ONE EXCAVATION

### Step 1 — Open your Daily Report
1. Open the MASCI Operations site on your phone.
2. Tap **Daily Reports** → **Start New Daily Report**.
3. Fill in the report exactly the way you always do — same way as last week.

### Step 2 — Answer the excavation question honestly
The Daily Report will ask: **"Excavation activity today?"**
* If you did NOT dig anything 4 ft or deeper today → tap **NO** and submit normally.
* If you DID dig today → tap **YES**. The form will then ask you to **Create or Link** an Excavation Record. Tap **Create New**.

### Step 3 — Fill out the Excavation Record
You'll see a single page. **You do not need to fill every field.** The form only asks for what it needs. Examples:
* If your trench is less than 4 ft deep, you'll skip half the form.
* If your trench is 5 ft+ deep, the form will ask about your protective system (trench box, sloping, etc.).

Take your time. The form coaches you — it is not a test.

### Step 4 — Pick your trench box and/or road plate
* Tap "Assigned Trench Safety Assets" → search for the box/plate you're using (e.g. **TB-03**, **RP-901**).
* Pick from the list. Don't type IDs by hand.

### Step 5 — Pick the Competent Person
* The form will ONLY show employees who are formally designated as a Competent Person.
* If you don't see the right name, tell your observer immediately — that's important feedback.

### Step 6 — Submit
* Look at the status box at the top. Green = Good. Orange = needs review. Red = action required.
* If red — read the box, fix what it asks (e.g. acknowledge a stacked-box exception), then submit.
* You can ALWAYS submit. The form never blocks you. Safety follows up.

### Step 7 — If something changes
If it rains, you hit a utility, the wall sloughs, or anything else changes during the day, **request a reinspection**:
1. Go back to your submission (success page or list).
2. Tap **Request Reinspection**.
3. Pick the reason that fits (Rain Event, Water Intrusion, Cave-In, Protective System Changed, Utility Conflict, Near Miss, Other).
4. Send. Safety and the Superintendent are notified instantly.

---

## DAY 2 · PRACTICE THE FAST PATH

* Open a new Daily Report.
* This time, when the excavation question pops up, try **Link Existing** instead of Create New — link yesterday's record to today's Daily Report. (You can also Create New if your excavation is genuinely new.)
* Fill out a second Excavation Record from scratch, but use a different trench box and a different soil type.

---

## DAY 3 · TRY TO BREAK IT

This is the most important day. **Try things the form might not handle**:
* Pick a trench box that's smaller than your trench (e.g. TB-03 in a 9 ft trench) — verify the form catches it.
* Use a road plate that's smaller than your opening — verify the form catches it.
* Skip the Competent Person — verify the form catches it.
* Switch languages mid-form.
* Lose cell signal and come back — does it remember your work?
* Submit an Emergency Excavation (set the toggle to Yes) — verify it shows up immediately on the Superintendent's view.

---

## WHAT WE'RE ASKING

Just three things:

1. **Use the form like you normally would.**
2. **When something is confusing, frustrating, slow, or wrong — tell your observer immediately.**
3. **At the end of each day, spend 5 minutes answering 8 questions in the Feedback Form.**

There are no wrong answers. We are testing the platform — not you.

---

## SAFETY REMINDER

* You always have **STOP-WORK AUTHORITY.** If anything looks wrong on the job site, stop the job. The form does not change that.
* If the form is slow or broken, **stop using the form and call Safety.** Do the job by paper if necessary.
* Coaching, not punishment. Any flag the form raises is so Safety can help — not so you get in trouble.

Thanks for running this trial. Your honest feedback is what makes the platform real.
