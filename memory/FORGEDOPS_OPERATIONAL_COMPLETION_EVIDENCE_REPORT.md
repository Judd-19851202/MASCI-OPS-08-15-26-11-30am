8. Scorecard derived from steps 1–7 → results in `OPERATIONAL_COMPLETION_SCORECARD.md`
9. Operator signs §11 of THIS document

## 12 · Post-certification operating doctrine

Once §11 is signed:
- The platform enters a **90-day independence trial**: zero engineering changes, observed-failures-only.
- Any failure during the trial is captured back into the Truth Register and routed through the FOCP Final Directive doctrine (7-test + 4-proof).
- White-Label and Customer #2 work may proceed in PARALLEL **only** if (a) §11 is signed AND (b) a new Truth Register entry per work item passes the gates.

If §11 cannot be signed by the operator on first review, the operator returns this document to the appropriate Phase artifact with a remediation directive. The AI agent then awaits that directive — it does NOT auto-promote.
