# PRE-DEPLOYMENT TRANSPORTATION ACCEPTANCE GATE

Date: 2026-08-11

## Core doctrine

- VISIBLE = USABLE
- HIDDEN does not count as accepted transportation functionality.
- A route, control, or workflow must be discoverable and operable from its intended portal surface to count as release-ready.

## Acceptance rules

- No admin-only transportation control may be represented as operator-usable.
- No hidden or stale link may stand in for a valid transportation experience.
- Dispatch, field, and admin transportation surfaces must preserve role scope and truthful status reporting.

## Current gate posture

- Admin-strict transportation audit surfaces remain governed.
- Transportation release inventory remains documented and unchanged by this pre-save hardening pass.
- Any future deployment must re-run transportation runtime and visibility proofs after owner Save.
