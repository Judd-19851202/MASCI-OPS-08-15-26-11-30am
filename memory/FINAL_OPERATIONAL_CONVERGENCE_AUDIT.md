## Executive summary — what we found

### 🔴 Critical (operational visibility breaks)
1. **PRODUCTION DRIFT — 24 iters undeployed.** Preview is iter353c+iter353b-availability complete. `mascidocs.com` is stuck somewhere before iter350. Concretely: `GET /api/hr/employees/{id}/accountability/timeline` → **404 in prod, 401-gated in preview**. Same for `/api/hr/employees/{id}/accountability/brief.pdf`, `/api/dispatch/driver-qualification`, and the iter352 CDL importer apply endpoint. **Every win since iter330 lives on preview only.** See `PRODUCTION_DRIFT_REPORT.md`.
2. **FL has DQ but no Training / PPE / Tasks / Incident visibility.** FL token returns **401** on `/api/safety/training-records`, `/api/safety-forms/equipment-issuances`, `/api/tasks`, `/api/incidents`, `/api/daily-reports`. iter353b gave FL Driver Readiness — but they cannot verify training currency, PPE compliance, or open tasks for the SAME driver they're about to send out. Operationally incomplete.
3. **HR cannot list incidents directly.** HR token returns 401 on `/api/incidents`. HR can see incidents ONLY via the iter353c accountability timeline, embedded in per-employee context. There is NO HR-wide incident list page. HR has no way to ask "show me all incidents this quarter" from inside HR portal.
4. **PM cannot list crew training or PPE.** PM token rejected on `/api/safety/training-records` and `/api/safety-forms/equipment-issuances`. PM is the closest accountability owner for crew on their jobs — yet they cannot verify training currency before assigning crew to a high-hazard task. Compliance contradiction.

### 🟡 Major (continuity weakness)
5. **Notification fan-out has no FL recipient role.** `recipient_role` enum is `safety · pm` based on observed `qaqc.deficiency` notifications. FL is field — they get nothing in their inbox today.
6. **FL no Daily Report / Tasks visibility.** Both 401. FL submits daily reports from the field but cannot inspect their own historical submissions from the portal surface.
7. **Dispatch no Daily Reports list.** 401. Dispatch needs to know yesterday's crew movement; falls back to dispatch-today snapshot only.

### 🟢 Solid (verified working)
- iter353c accountability timeline aggregates training · PPE · incidents · FL records · CDL · medical · status_history correctly. 43 incidents in preview reachable for employee-scoped queries.
- iter353b parity verified: Dispatch + FL see identical DQ counts via shared helper.
