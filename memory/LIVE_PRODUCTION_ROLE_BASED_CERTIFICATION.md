## 🟡 PHASE 9 VERDICT — OPERATOR-SIDE PER-ROLE WALKTHROUGH REQUIRED

Role-based UX evaluation is inherently human-side: it requires logging in as each persona, completing the persona's primary task, and judging whether the experience is clear. The agent cannot do this without manufacturing production identities (which is forbidden). The structured per-role evaluation grid is provided below.

## 2 · Acceptance criteria

- For every role, the operator should be able to answer "YES" to Q1, Q2, Q3 and "NO" to Q4.
- A "NO" on Q1/Q2/Q3 or "YES" on Q4 indicates a UX friction point — classify per Phase 10 severity.
- Particularly important for laborer and foreman (the highest-volume roles): the platform must be usable on a phone at 6:30 AM with gloves on.
