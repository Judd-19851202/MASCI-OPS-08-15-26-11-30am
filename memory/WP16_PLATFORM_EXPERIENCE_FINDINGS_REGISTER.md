## Constitutional note
This register is the first complete findings register for the WP-16 census phase. It is intentionally platform-wide and non-exempt. Additional per-screen findings may be appended as each route family is remediated in detail, but the inventory and these systemic findings already establish the non-compliant surface areas that must be addressed before WP-16 can be declared complete.
