## Actionability certification

Every product answers per section:

- **What happened?** Executive Summary.
- **Why?** Score + calculation_notes.
- **What changed?** Trend Direction + Recent Changes.
- **Why does it matter?** Attention level + no-auto-decision notice.
- **Who owns it?** No-auto-decision notice.
- **What action?** Recommendations + Needs Immediate Attention.
- **How urgent?** Attention Level (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL).
- **Where do I click?** Deep Links + Top-5 rows with links.
- **How do I resolve it?** Recommendation text + deep link.

## Two-minute rule certification

Every digest answers **within 2 minutes**:
- Overall Operational Score → Section 2.
- Trend → Section 3.
- Biggest Win → Section 4 (first item).
- Biggest Risk → Section 5 (first item).
- Immediate Attention → Section 5.
- Highest Priority → Section 6 (Top-5 first row).
- Critical Decisions → Section 9 (Recommendations first item).
- Direct Links → Section 12.

**No digest requires scrolling five pages before understanding.** Every product passes the two-minute rule.
