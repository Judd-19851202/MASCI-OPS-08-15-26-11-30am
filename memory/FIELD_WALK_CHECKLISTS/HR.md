# FIELD WALK · HR / OFFICE

_Operator role: HR officer / accounting · Device: laptop · Time: ~10 min._

---

## 1 · HR Hub entry

1. Sign in to HR portal.
2. PROVES: HR Hub loads.
3. PROVES: governance chip green / improving.

## 2 · PO Issue & Receipt flow

1. Tap **PO Requests**.
2. Open an approved PO with no PO# yet.
3. PROVES: Approval block visible (you have approver caps).
4. Enter a Manual PO #.
5. Re-approve.
6. PROVES: PO # appears on the PO record.

## 3 · Receipt review

1. Open a PO in `Receipt Uploaded` status.
2. PROVES: receipt thumbnail or download link visible.
3. PROVES: amount + vendor + project number all visible together.

## 4 · Notifications

1. Open the bell.
2. PROVES: "PO needs approval" tasks (cc).
3. PROVES: payroll variance batches surface (if any).

## 5 · Payroll variance batch

1. Open the payroll variance tile.
2. PROVES: pending batches visible.
3. Open a batch.
4. PROVES: each row has approve / reject / note actions.
5. PROVES: decision is recorded with timestamp + actor name.

## 6 · Capability scope

1. Open `/po-requests`.
2. PROVES: NO **Cancel** button (admin-only).
3. PROVES: NO **Close** button (admin-only).
4. PROVES: Approve / Clarify / Reject / Manual PO # / Approved
   amount ARE visible.

## Pass / Fail

Same as other roles — any miss is a ticket with Support ID.
