# WP18 ECAP Compatibility and Transition Plan

Date: 2026-08-03

## Compatibility law

**Decision label:** `APPROVED_CONSTITUTIONAL_DECISION`  
**Proof label:** `DOCUMENTED_ONLY`

WP-18C must evolve the platform without breaking validated operator workflows.

## Transition rules

1. Existing portals and shells remain the primary entry structure.
2. New budget/forecast/EV functionality must appear as additive governed capabilities inside existing role paths.
3. Legacy executive digest capabilities may retire only after equivalent governed replacements are active.
4. Historical records remain accessible across transition.
5. Compatibility must be verified at API, workflow, permission, report, and mobile/responsive levels.

## Transition result

Compatibility and transition behavior are fully decided and do not block WP-18C.
