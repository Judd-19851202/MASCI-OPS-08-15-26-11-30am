# TRACK 19.40 · INDUSTRY COMPARISON

Reviewed against HCSS · Procore · Raken · Autodesk Build · Power BI Executive Dashboards · Fleetio · Samsara · Motive · SafetyCulture · Buildertrend.

## Extracted best ideas (adopted)
- **Fleetio / Samsara:** per-domain digest surfaces (fleet, transportation) — adopted as first-class registered products with locked contracts (`fleet_intelligence`, `transportation_intelligence`).
- **HCSS HeavyJob:** operational rollup per project — adopted as `project_intelligence` product.
- **Power BI Executive Dashboards:** trend arrows + previous vs. current — adopted in `trend.compute_trend`.
- **SafetyCulture:** "attention signal only" language + no-auto-decision doctrine — already in Tracks 19.37/19.39, preserved here.
- **Autodesk Build / Raken:** deep-linked citations from every metric — engine renderer supports `{href, text}` cells natively.

## Explicitly rejected (fails Six-Pillars)
- **Cross-tenant leaderboards** (SafetyCulture-style) — fails Trusted (privacy risk across tenants).
- **AI-authored briefing paragraphs** (BI-vendor auto-summarize) — fails No-Auto-Decision doctrine.
- **In-email interactive forms** (Slack/Teams-style approvals) — fails Simple; forms belong in the platform, not the email.
- **Read-receipt pixel tracking** (marketing-tool style) — fails Trusted; audit records only what the provider gives us.

## Verdict
The engine matches or exceeds the operational products of every competitor listed while explicitly refusing the anti-patterns that would burn trust or leak decisions.
