# WP16 Operator Experience Constitution

## Purpose
WP-16 defines the platform experience constitution for the MASCI operating system. The goal is not cosmetic modernization. The goal is to make the software disappear so operators can complete work with minimal cognitive effort while preserving every WP-15 governance guarantee.

## Constitutional intent
- Field-first, operations-first, mobile-first.
- Every major screen must be understandable within 5 seconds.
- Every feature must have one obvious canonical home.
- No module may invent new interaction patterns outside the shared design system.
- Every material action must preserve Trust Spine visibility and evidence-first confirmation.

## Operator questions every screen must answer
1. Where am I?
2. What am I looking at?
3. What needs my attention?
4. What can I do next?
5. How do I leave this screen?

## Non-negotiable standards
- Navigation Escape Rule: every workflow must expose an obvious path to go back, cancel, close, or return home.
- Operator Cognitive Load Rule: avoid hidden controls, ambiguous labels, duplicate entry, and decorative complexity.
- Golden Path Rule: the most common path must be the easiest path.
- Evidence Rule: confirmations and important errors must explain what changed, why, and what to do next.
- Sunlight Rule: no low-contrast surfaces, vague states, or glass-heavy overlays that degrade legibility.

## Execution order
1. Constitutional foundation
2. Platform shell and information architecture
3. Shared platform experience standardization
4. Operator coaching and content audit
5. Workflow optimization
6. Measurement and regression protection

## Deliverables required by this constitution
- Canonical visual language
- Canonical interaction model
- Canonical information architecture
- Canonical iconography standard
- Canonical platform copy and coaching standard
- Shared UX components under `/app/frontend/src/design-system/`

## Complete platform experience census
WP-16 requires a complete constitutional census of the entire MASCI Operations Platform before major interface modernization proceeds.

- No user-accessible page, route, workflow, drawer, dialog, modal, search surface, report, settings screen, or responsive layout is exempt.
- Representative sampling is constitutionally insufficient.
- Every discovered surface must be inventoried.
- Every inventoried surface must be reviewed against WP-16.
- Every applicable finding must be corrected, accepted with rationale, or explicitly deferred with justification.

The working census artifacts for this phase are recorded in:
- `/app/memory/WP16_PLATFORM_EXPERIENCE_CENSUS.md`
- `/app/memory/WP16_PLATFORM_EXPERIENCE_FINDINGS_REGISTER.md`
- `/app/memory/WP16_PLATFORM_SURFACE_INVENTORY.json`

## Platform personality
The platform must feel:
- professional
- confident
- industrial
- modern
- calm
- trustworthy

It must never feel generic, trendy for its own sake, or assembled from unrelated admin templates.
